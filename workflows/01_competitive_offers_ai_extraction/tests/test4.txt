case
  when upper(stg.Campaign_Category) = 'DISCOVERY' then 'Programmatic Display'
  when charindex('BRAND', upper(stg.Campaign_Name)) > 0 then 'Branded Paid Search'
  else 'Non-Branded Paid Search'
end as Media_Tactic
 
Discovery campaigns (Campaign_Category = 'Discovery') are not in the search split. They are labeled Programmatic Display.
If the campaign name contains the substring BRAND (case-insensitive), the row is Branded Paid Search.
Every other SA360 row is Non-Branded Paid Search.


SELECT
  TMM_Category,
  CASE WHEN CONTAINS_SUBSTR(UPPER(TMM_Campaign_Desc), 'BRAND') THEN 'Matches BRAND substring' ELSE 'No match' END AS substring_test,
  COUNT(*) AS rows,
  SUM(Spend) AS spend
FROM `prj-dbi-prd-1.ds_dbi_marketing.media_analytics_integrated_summary`
WHERE UPPER(TRIM(LOB)) = 'POSTPAID'
  AND UPPER(TRIM(Channel)) = 'PAID SEARCH'
  AND Date >= DATE_SUB(CURRENT_DATE(), INTERVAL 90 DAY)
GROUP BY TMM_Category, substring_test
ORDER BY spend DESC;
