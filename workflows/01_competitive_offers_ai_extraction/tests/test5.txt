In Databricks, a Notification Destination is a configuration that tells Databricks where to send alerts/notifications when certain events happen.
Common destinations include:
Email — send notifications to one or more email addresses
Microsoft Teams — send messages to a Teams channel/webhook
Slack — send notifications to Slack
Webhooks — send an HTTP request to another application/service
Other supported notification integrations depending on your Databricks environment
