MFC Forecast Data — Quick Summary (updated)

1. Missing forecasts
Two real gaps found, and they're different in kind:

July 4, 2026 — a source data behavior, not a bug. The campaigns for that week are still tagged by the source as "Q2" even in the latest file, so their spend lands on the Q2 quarter-end date instead of Q3. The money isn't lost, just filed under the old quarter label until the source updates the tag.
January 3, 2026 — a confirmed pipeline bug, now root-caused. A real ~$5.0M forecast exists for this week in the source, but one specific file (loaded Dec 26, 2025) has a corrupted internal date field. Because of how our matching logic compares that field, the entire file's data was being silently dropped rather than used. Fix identified; rolling it out now, and checking whether other weeks were affected the same way.

Outside these two dates, forecast data is present and consistent across the full window checked.

2. Channels/tactics shifting between file loads
Individual channels and tactics do come and go week to week in the raw files. To keep reporting reliable despite that, the pipeline always includes every channel/tactic combination ever seen for every date — even weeks where a combination has no spend get an explicit blank row instead of disappearing entirely. This keeps week-over-week comparisons accurate and makes any gap visible instead of silent.

3. OTT example — why our forecast showed ~$27M instead of ~$24M
Three OTT campaigns (worth ~$2.63M) disappeared from one file, then reappeared the next week split into three new, more specific tactic names. Same real money, just renamed. Since our system tracks campaigns by their exact name, it counted both the old name (carried forward as "still active") and the new names — double-counting the same spend. Once found, we corrected it, and the forecast now reports the accurate ~$24M figure.

SELECT Quarter, COUNT(*) AS rows, SUM(weekly_forecast) AS total
FROM prdrzranalytics.lab42.sdi_tbl_mfc_bronze_spendForecastGranular_weekly
WHERE QGP_Week = '2026-01-03' AND LOB_Supported = 'CONSUMER POSTPAID'
GROUP BY Quarter;

SELECT TRY_CAST(CAST(FileLoad_Date AS STRING) AS DATE) AS FileLoad_Date, Quarter, SUM(Spend) AS total
FROM prdrzranalytics.lab42.raw_media_flowchart
WHERE UPPER(TRIM(LOB_Supported)) = 'TFB'
  AND UPPER(TRIM(QGP)) = 'FORECAST'
  AND TRY_CAST(QGP_Week AS DATE) = '2020-07-04'
GROUP BY TRY_CAST(CAST(FileLoad_Date AS STRING) AS DATE), Quarter
ORDER BY FileLoad_Date;
