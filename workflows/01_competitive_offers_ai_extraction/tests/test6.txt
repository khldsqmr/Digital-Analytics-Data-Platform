Currently, I’m planning to define Biddable Spend like this. For each source, below is the full universe available and the subset we would use:

Programmatic (pbi_programmatic_browsers_currentyr)
Universe: Channel = Display, OLV, Streaming Radio, plus some NULL; DSP = Amazon DSP, Blis, DV360, The Trade Desk; LOBs include PostPaid, HSI, PrePaid, TFB, Archived.
Our selection: Include all DSPs and all channel values, but restrict to Postpaid + HSI/Broadband only.
Excluded: PrePaid, TFB, Archived.
Paid Social (media_analytics_integrated_snapshot)
Universe: Channel_Group_Name = 'Paid Social'; Channel_Name includes Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest, Twitter, LinkedIn, Indirect, Nextdoor, General MFC, etc.; agencies include InHouse, Initiative, Assurance, TMO Accessibility.
Our selection: Agency = 'InHouse' + Postpaid/Broadband only, and include all qualifying social platforms underneath that scope.
Excluded: Initiative/Assurance/Accessibility and non-PP/BB LOBs. This means things like Indirect, Nextdoor and General MFC currently fall out because they sit under Initiative.
Paid Search (sdi_tbl_sa360_gold_campaign_daily)
Universe: ad_platform = Google, Bing; advertising_channel_type = SEARCH, SHOPPING, PERFORMANCE_MAX, DISCOVERY; campaign_type = Brand, Generic, Shopping, PMax, DemandGen; LOBs include Postpaid, HSI, Metro, TFB, Fiber.
Our selection: Include Google + Bing and all campaign/channel types, restricted to Postpaid + HSI/Broadband only.
Excluded: Metro, TFB, Fiber and other non-PP/BB LOBs.
